# Persona doc template

One to two screens. Roles and company types only: no names, emails or profile links anywhere in this file.

```markdown
# Who signed up for <what it was>

> **In short:** The average sign-up is <a seniority + function at a size + industry company in a region>.
> **Based on:** <n> researched of <total> rows (<sampled or full list>). <n> dropped as junk or duplicates.
> **Confidence:** <strong / mixed / weak>, because <the main unknown rate>.
> Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>

## The spread

**Seniority:** <top 2 or 3 buckets with %>. Unknown <x%>.
**Function:** <...>. Unknown <x%>.
**Industry:** <...>. Unknown <x%>.
**Company size:** <...>. Unknown <x%>.
**Region:** <...>. Unknown <x%>.

## What stands out
- <a pattern worth acting on, for example "a third are solo founders, more than the webinar topic suggested">
- <a surprise or a gap>

## Compared with your ICP
<only if an ICP was found: share of the list that looks like the ICP, and the biggest gap. Otherwise leave this section out.>

## How sure we are
<what is self-reported, what is inferred from research, and which dimension is weakest. One short paragraph.>
```

Writing rules: plain, short sentences, no em dashes. Numbers are rounded percentages with the count when it's small ("4 people, 8%").

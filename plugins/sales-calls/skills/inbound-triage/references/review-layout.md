# Review layout and drafts

## Layout

One weekly review, numbered straight through, so you can answer "draft 2 and 5, move 7, close 9".

1. **Counts:** per channel and offer category, last 7 days. How many answered and not answered. Open leads checked.
2. **New opportunities:** Name | Company | Channel | Offer | Interest | Their ask (short quote) | Status | Recommended action (log at which stage, reply or not).
3. **Needs you:** replies and follow-ups recommended, each with one line of why. No draft text yet.
4. **Proposed moves:** stage changes with evidence, including closes and reopens.
5. **Open leads with nothing to do:** one line with the count. Details only if asked.
6. **Flagged, not logged:** partnerships, collaborations and similar, one line each.
7. **Not checked:** any channel skipped or failed, and how far paging got.

Every lead in sections 2 to 4 has exactly one recommended next action.

## Example (made-up)

Opening:

"Last 7 days: Email 5 (Service 2, Product 2, Sponsorship 1), LinkedIn 3 (Product 2, Service 1), Booking 1, Form 2. Not answered: 3. Open leads checked: 21, 6 need you."

New opportunity:

"1. Dana | Acme, about 120 people | Email | Service | High | 'Can you send pricing for 30 people?' | Needs reply | Log at In conversation, reply today"

Needs you:

"4. Reply to Dana: she asked for pricing for 30 people.
5. Follow-up 2 to Leo (Kito): proposal is with his manager, 5 days since your last nudge."

Proposed moves:

"7. Priya (Brightlane): Waiting to Lost, 3 follow-ups with no answer since Sep 28."

Flagged, not logged:

"Marco: proposes a revenue-share partnership on his newsletter. Waiting for your answer."

## Draft rules

Use your voice skill or the samples you gave. Short sentences. No em dashes or double dashes. Clean direct links.

- **Reply:** answer their exact question first. One link at most. One next step.
- **Follow-up 1:** a short nudge plus one useful thing (an answer, a resource, a relevant example).
- **Follow-up 2:** one new fact or angle. Don't repeat follow-up 1.
- **Follow-up 3:** close the loop. Leave the door open and say it's the last one.

Facts in drafts:

- Links, prices, dates and offer details come only from your context or what you tell me. Check them before writing.
- Never invent a discount code or a deadline. If you want one in and it isn't in context, ask for it.
- Never pretend to be anyone other than you.

Where drafts go:

- **Email:** a draft that replies to the latest real message in the thread (not a tracking notification), with the same recipients and cc.
- **LinkedIn and other DMs:** text in the chat, ready to paste.
- Nothing is sent.

## Saving

Offer to save the weekly review to `outputs/inbound/YYYY-MM-DD-inbound-review.md`. In a scheduled run with nobody there, save it without drafting or logging anything.

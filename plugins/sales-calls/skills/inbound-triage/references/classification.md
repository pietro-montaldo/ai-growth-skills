# Classification

## Bucket

**Opportunity.** Someone who would pay you for one of your offers (from your context). For example:

- a person buying your product, course or plan
- a company asking for your service, project, retainer, training or consulting
- a brand or agency paying you for reach (sponsored post, newsletter placement, sponsored event), if you sell that

Tag each opportunity with the offer category from your setup.

**Flagged, not logged.** Worth one line in the report, never logged as a lead:

- partnerships, collaborations, co-selling, resale or referral splits
- affiliate programs and revenue-share offers
- advisor or board roles, guest or volunteer sessions, podcast invites
- people asking for feedback, advice or a favour, with no buying ask
- existing clients handling contract, billing, scheduling or delivery logistics
- catch-up calls with no buying ask

**Noise.** Ignore: vendors selling to you, recruiters, newsletters, automated notifications, "great post" messages.

If a booking or message could be either, read the thread. Still unsure: flag it and say why.

## Interest (opportunities only)

- **High:** a specific ask: dates, price, team size, budget, a booked call, or a strong fit with your ICP.
- **Medium:** genuine interest, still vague.
- **Low:** polite curiosity.

## Fit and best offer

For people not yet in your pipeline, check them against your ICP: right kind of company or person, right size, right situation. If you sell several offers, pick the one that fits best, which may differ from what they asked for. A "team" ask from a 3-person company might fit a smaller offer better. Say so in the report.

## Match to existing leads

Match by email, then LinkedIn URL, then name, then company. Also check Won and Lost leads: a Lost lead who is back is worth a proposal to reopen.

One person counts once, even if they wrote on several channels. Merge.

## Status (read from the thread, not from labels)

- **Needs reply:** they wrote last.
- **Waiting:** you wrote last, and no follow-up is due yet.
- **Follow-up due:** you wrote last, you have sent fewer than 3 follow-ups, and the gap has passed: 3 days before follow-up 1, 5 more days before follow-up 2, 7 more days before follow-up 3.
- **Closed no response:** 3 follow-ups and no answer. Propose moving to Lost.
- **Done:** nothing open (they booked, bought, declined, or the question was fully answered).

Counting follow-ups: your messages after their last one, minus your first reply.

Your own follow-up timing can differ. If your context sets a different cadence, use it.

## Stages (for a lead log, or to map to your CRM)

Lead, In conversation, Call booked, Proposal sent, Negotiation, Waiting (later date or internal approval), Won (signed or paid), Lost (declined, not a fit, or 3 follow-ups with no answer).

If your CRM has its own stages, use those and map these ideas onto them. Don't add new stages to someone's CRM.

# Channels

Collect only the channels you chose in setup. Each one has a fallback, so no channel blocks the run. Your own addresses are the domains in your context: messages from them are "you".

## Email (Gmail, Outlook or similar)

Search the last 7 days and page until results run out. Build the searches from your offers in context. A typical set:

- Buying words for each offer: its name, plus words like price, pricing, cost, quote, proposal, demo, trial, "for my team", "how does it work", enroll, book, sign up.
- Service words, if you sell a service: project, help with, consulting, agency, retainer, training, workshop.
- Sponsorship words, if you sell reach: sponsor, sponsored, promote, "media kit", rates, placement.
- Replies to you: inbox threads with "Re:" in the subject, not from you.
- For every open lead with an email: messages from or to that address in the last 7 days.

Leave out promotions and automated folders where your email tool supports it.

Dedupe by thread. Open every candidate thread before classifying: search results often show only the oldest messages, so the latest one is missing. Read the latest message in full for anything you'll classify as an opportunity.

**Fallback:** paste the threads, or forward them into one document and share it.

## LinkedIn DMs

**With a LinkedIn messages connector (read only):** list recent conversations and page until threads are older than 7 days. Skip threads where your own automated message is the only one. Open each candidate thread by the exact person ID the list returns (a first name can match several people). Don't enrich or look anyone up. If the connector refuses, mark LinkedIn "not checked".

**Fallback:** paste the conversations (copy the thread text from LinkedIn), or upload a LinkedIn data export (`messages.csv` from "Get a copy of your data"). From an export, read only rows from the last 7 days.

## Booking tool (cal.com, Calendly, SavvyCal or similar)

**With a connector:** list bookings created in the last 7 days, upcoming and past. Read the title, notes and the booking form answers.

**Fallback:** export the bookings list as CSV, or paste the confirmation emails.

A booking is an opportunity only when the booking itself or a matching thread shows a buying ask. A catch-up with a friend or an existing client is flagged or ignored.

## Forms (website contact form, Typeform, Tally, Google Forms, Webflow or similar)

**With a connector:** list submissions from the last 7 days and read every answer.

**Fallback:** export the responses as CSV and put it in your working folder, or paste the notification emails.

## Anything else

WhatsApp, Slack communities, X or Instagram DMs, a shared inbox: paste the messages. Treat them like DMs.

## Big weeks

More than about 40 candidate threads: start one subagent per channel, in parallel. Ask each to return a compact table: person, company, channel, date of latest message, who wrote last, the ask in one short quote, and the thread reference. Merge the tables in the main run.

## What to record per channel for the report

How many threads or entries were read, how far paging got, and whether the channel was checked, partly checked or not checked.

# Delivery email

Written after the proposal is approved. If past proposal emails are in your working folder, match the most recent one. Otherwise use this structure.

## Structure

1. **Opener:** one warm line about the call. If you promised the recording or a link, include it. Then: "The proposal is attached. The detailed plan follows once you pick a direction." (Drop the second sentence if there's no held-back scope.)
2. **The logic, not an essay per option:** two or three short paragraphs, each starting with a bold lead, that explain the option families. What "customised" means. What per-team adds and why there are fewer tracks than teams. Then the options as a bare numbered list with prices.
3. **Add-ons:** one paragraph with the logic, for example "a shared option plus the support pack is the lower-priced alternative to per-team". One-line recommendation inside it, if you want to recommend.
4. **Proof (optional):** "For your reference:" with cleared client links, results or your website. Adapt the framing to the buyer's real situation (for example, "if it helps when you present this to your leadership team").
5. **Comparison line (optional):** only if verified per `pricing-ladder.md`: "up to X% less per person than [public price]", with the public link.
6. **Spoken-number lines:** one line for any number that changed since the call.
7. **Reserved line (optional):** "These options and prices were built for your team. If you're comparing providers, please keep them between us."
8. **Close:** "Let me know which works best." Offer to adjust, add detail, or take a 10 to 15 minute call to finalise around the option they lean toward.
9. **Optional:** one forward-looking line about working together longer, only when there's a real angle.

No timing paragraph unless you ask. Availability lives in the proposal's next steps.

## Voice

Your voice skill or the samples you gave. Short sentences. No em dashes or double dashes. No filler. Match the formality of the call.

## Mechanics

- Show the full draft in chat and wait for approval.
- Check every link opens the site it claims to. Pasted links sometimes point to an email client or a tracking wrapper instead.
- With an email connector, create the draft as a reply in the latest thread with the client. Without one, give the text to paste.
- Most email connectors can't attach files. Say plainly: "Attach the proposal file before sending. Don't attach the detailed plan yet."
- Never send.

> Hi Dana,
>
> Thanks again for the time on Tuesday. Here's the recording as promised: [link]. The proposal is attached. The detailed plan follows once you pick a direction.
>
> **Standard or customised.** The standard options use our proven playbook. The customised options rebuild every example around your own pipeline and tools, which is why they cost more per hour.
>
> **Per team.** Everyone starts together, then sales and success split into their own tracks. Two tracks instead of four keeps the price friendly and still gives each team what it needs.
>
> 1. Core: 6,000
> 2. Full: 10,800
> 3. Full, tailored: 14,400

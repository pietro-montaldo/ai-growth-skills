# Email templates

Two layouts. Adjust the wording to your voice; keep the order. Paragraphs are separated by a blank line.

## Attendee version

```
# Webinar follow-up: [Session title] (attendees)

**Audience:** People who attended "[Session title]"
**Session date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]

## Subject options

1. Recording and slides: [Short session title]
2. [A takeaway-led subject, under 60 characters]

## Body

Hi [first name, or "there"],

Thanks for joining "[Session title]" today. Here are the resources:

**Recording:** [link]
**Slides:** [link]
[**Other resource:** [link], one line per extra resource promised]

The [3 to 5] things to take away:

- [Takeaway 1]
- [Takeaway 2]
- [Takeaway 3]

[Optional offer block]

Any question about the session, just reply to this email.

[Sign-off],
[Your name, optionally linked]
```

## No-show version

```
# Webinar follow-up: [Session title] (no-shows)

**Audience:** People who registered for "[Session title]" and didn't attend
**Session date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]

## Subject options

1. You missed it live, here's the recording
2. [A curiosity subject built on the best takeaway or audience question]

## Body

Hi [first name, or "there"],

[One line, no guilt: "Live sessions are hard to fit in, so here's the recording of [Session title]."]

**Recording:** [link]
**Slides:** [link]

What's inside:

- [Takeaway 1, framed as what they'll learn]
- [Takeaway 2]
- [The best audience question, if there was one: "Plus the question everyone asked: [question]"]

[Optional offer block]

If you watch it and have a question, just reply.

[Sign-off],
[Your name, optionally linked]
```

## Offer block (optional)

Only when you asked for one. Built from your offer in context, matched to the session topic. Two or three sentences, then one link line.

```
[Bridge: one sentence linking the session topic to the offer. "If you want to go from [what the session covered] to [the outcome your offer delivers], that's what [Offer name] is for."]

[Who it's for, in one sentence.]

[Incentive line, only if you supplied one, with its real terms: "Attendees get **[incentive]** until [real date]."]

[Call to action]: [link]
```

Rules for the block:

- One call to action per offer. Clear link text ("See the full programme", "Book a call"), not "click here".
- An incentive only if you gave one. Its framing is honest and positive (for example "for people who joined live", "first group", "until Friday" when Friday is real). Never fake urgency.
- At most two offers, and only if you asked for two. Same shape for each.
- No prices, codes, ratings or review counts unless you supplied them.

## Length and style

- Body 120 to 220 words.
- Subjects under 60 characters where possible.
- Short sentences. No em dashes or double dashes. No corporate words ("leverage", "unlock", "excited to announce").
- Bold only the resource labels and an incentive.

## Example (made-up)

Session: "Pricing Your First Retainer", hosted by a consultant called Alex.

Attendee subject: `Recording and slides: Pricing Your First Retainer`

```
Hi there,

Thanks for joining "Pricing Your First Retainer" today. Here are the resources:

**Recording:** [link]
**Slides:** [link]
**Retainer scoping checklist:** [link]

The 3 things to take away:

- Price the outcome, then check it against your hours. Not the other way round.
- Offer three levels of support, so the middle one feels like the safe choice.
- Put the review date in the first contract, so raising the price later isn't awkward.

If you want help building your own retainer offer, that's what the Retainer Lab is for. It's a 4-week small group for consultants moving from projects to monthly work.

See the full programme: [link]

Any question about the session, just reply to this email.

Talk soon,
Alex
```

In a real email, every `[link]` is the actual URL you supplied.

# Inputs checklist

Collect every item that applies before writing the body. A draft full of `[LINK]` and `[DATE]` gets sent with gaps in it, and that costs reader trust. If something is missing, ask for all of it in one message.

## Always needed

- **The main piece and its link.** The one thing most readers should click: a guide, template, video, article, tool, recording or offer page. Without it the edition has no main click and isn't ready.
- **What the main piece is.** Its source text (a post, the guide, a transcript) or enough detail to describe it: what problem it solves, what's inside, how long it takes, what the result looks like.
- **Edition type.** Teaching or roundup (see `edition-types.md`).
- **Promote or only teach.** Does this edition promote something? If yes, which offer.
- **Edition number or title**, if your newsletter uses them. Check your past editions or ask.
- **Sign-off.** From your past editions. If there are none, ask once and save it with your profile.

## Needed when they appear in the edition

- **Events** (webinar, workshop, live session, office hours): title, link, date, time and time zone, read from the event page. Never write "see the page for timing". Say if it's free.
- **Guest speakers**: name spelled the way they spell it, role and company, one credential line they'd be happy to see. Confirm it with you. Never pull a credential from memory.
- **Offer block** (only when the edition promotes): what it is in one line, who it's for, the link, and any real deadline, start date or limited spots. A discount only if you give the exact amount and end date.
- **Secondary items** in a roundup: one link and one line of why it matters for each.
- **Video**: the link and what it covers, or a transcript.
- **"Next time" topics**: 1 to 3, from you. Skip the section if you have none. Never invent them.
- **Proof** you want to mention (results, sign-up counts, downloads, replies): the exact number and where it comes from.

## How to ask

Use one block, headed "Before I draft, I need:", then a short bulleted list of only the missing items. Group related items ("For the workshop: date, time zone, guest's role"). Don't start the body until each item is answered or you say to drop it.

## Where to look before asking

- Your GTM context or company profile: the offer, its link, who reads.
- Your past editions: numbering, labels, sign-off, usual sections.
- Links you gave: open them for dates, titles, chapter lists and setup time.
